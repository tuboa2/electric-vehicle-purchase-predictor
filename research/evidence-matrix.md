# Research Evidence Matrix: Autonomous Kaggle Multi-Agent System

This matrix synthesizes and evaluates technical claims extracted from the 11 independent research documents (`claude.md`, `deepseek.md`, `gemini.md`, `glm.md`, `gpt.md`, `grok.md`, `julius.md`, `kimi.md`, `meta.md`, `mistral.md`, `qwen.md`).

### Evidence Classification Tiers
- **A — Directly Verified:** Confirmed against live CLI binary inspection, official platform docs, or reproducible code execution.
- **B — Strongly Supported:** Corroborated across 5+ independent research models, peer-reviewed literature, or established Kaggle Grandmaster consensus.
- **C — Plausible:** Supported by credible logic or 2–4 models, but lacks universal empirical verification or depends on specific problem types.
- **D — Speculative:** Unverified hypothesis, unbenchmarked claims, or theoretical proposals lacking empirical validation.
- **E — Unsupported / Disproven:** Contradicted by evidence, platform behavior, or empirical benchmarks.
